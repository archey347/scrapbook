This is a made-up grid, not the OS one. Lines are numbered 00–99 east and north from the origin, so a 6-figure ref is EEe NNn: the two-digit line number plus tenths across the square. Clues store their real position, so changing the grid just changes their refs — reprint any sheets afterwards.
