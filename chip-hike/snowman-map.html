Grid squares are 100 m. To find a 6-figure grid reference, go along the corridor, then up the stairs. The first two digits are the blue line number along the top/bottom (eastings). The third digit is how many tenths of the way across the square. The last three digits are the same, using the numbers up the side (northings). For example, 284 231 means 4/10 of the way east of line 28 and 1/10 of the way north of line 23. This is a made-up grid, so it won't match an OS map.
